How to play
  • FACEOFF: tap any button the instant the puck drops. Too early and you lose it. Aim the pad to choose where you draw it.
  • PAD skate. You always control the skater with the gold ring. On a computer: WASD or arrows, Space shoots, Shift dekes, E passes, Esc pauses.
  • SHOOT hold to wind up, let go to fire. Push the pad left or right to pick a corner. Push it toward the net as you let go to go top shelf.
  • BREAKAWAY: get past every defender and the goalie comes out to challenge. Deke him and finish.
  • PASS goes to the open teammate, or the one you're pointing at. Teammates show their job above them; gold OPEN! means they're in a scoring spot with a clean lane.
  • DEKE side-steps and drags the puck across. Point the pad left or right to choose the side. Pokes and checks can't touch you mid-deke.
  • ARENA MOVES each get their own button above the pad (keys Z X C V on a computer): Arena 2 SAUCER pass floats over sticks. Arena 3 SPIN-o-rama can't be checked and freezes the goalie. Arena 4 BOMB is a short wind-up into a huge slapshot. Arena 5 MICHIGAN works low beside their net and lights up gold when you're in the spot.
  • BACKHAND: tap SHOOT right after a deke, or while skating away from the net, for an instant shot the goalie reads late.
  • On defence: POKE reaches out with the stick, CHECK lunges for a body hit, SWITCH changes skaters.
  • ICING: clear the puck from your half past their goal line and the faceoff comes back to your zone. OFFSIDE: the puck must cross the blue line before you do.
  • EMOTES: tap the speech bubble on the rink's edge (or T, then 1-9). You start with 3 and find more in Epic, Mythic and Legendary star drops.
  • SHOOTOUT: three shooters each, then sudden death. One chance per attempt, no rebounds.
